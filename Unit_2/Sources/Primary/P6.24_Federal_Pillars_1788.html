Primary Source P6.24
The Federal Pillars: “The Federal Edifice”
Benjamin Russell, printer, Massachusetts Centinel (Boston) · August 2, 1788
 
TOC FIT Chapter 6 · Essay prompt 6.2 (Prophets or Obstructionists? The Ratification Debate)
WHY HERE Russell’s running “pillars” cartoon shows how Federalist printers presented ratification as the raising of a national structure, and how they treated the states still holding out.
Historical Context

Beginning in January 1788, Boston printer Benjamin Russell used a woodcut of pillars in his Federalist newspaper, the Massachusetts Centinel, to track ratification. Each state that ratified became a pillar raised under the “federal superstructure.” On June 25, 1788, after New Hampshire became the ninth state to ratify, he printed the “NINTH and the SUFFICIENT PILLAR,” marking the point at which the Constitution could take effect.

This later version, printed on August 2, 1788, after Virginia and New York ratified, shows eleven pillars. A hand labeled North Carolina’s pillar is being raised with the confident “Rise it will,” while Rhode Island’s lies broken: “The foundation good—it may yet be SAVED.” The accompanying verse promises that arts, agriculture, commerce, religion, and justice will flourish under the new union.

Editorial note: The unit proposal names the “Ninth Pillar” issue of June 25, 1788 (described by the Center for the Study of the American Constitution, https://csac.history.wisc.edu/states-and-ratification/new-hampshire/the-ninth-pillar/). No reusable image of that issue was located, so this reader reproduces the same series’ August 2, 1788 cartoon from the Library of Congress exhibition “Creating the United States.” The transcription was made by the compiler from the digital image; the full newspaper page and an enlarged detail are shown.
Document Text

REDEUNT SATURNIA REGNA.

On the erection of the Eleventh PILLAR of the great National DOME, we beg leave most sincerely to felicitate “OUR DEAR COUNTRY.”

[Pillars labeled:] DEL. — PEN. — N. JER. — GEO. — CON. — MASSA. — MARY. — S. CARO. — N. HAMP. — VIRG. —N. YORK. [A twelfth pillar, labeled N. CARO., is being raised: “Rise it will.” A broken pillar, labeled R. ISLAND: “The foundation good—it may yet be SAVED.”]

The FEDERAL EDIFICE.

ELEVEN STARS, in quick succession rise—
ELEVEN COLUMNS strike our wond’ring eyes,
Soon o’er the whole, shall swell the beauteous DOME,
COLUMBIA’s boast—and FREEDOM’s hallow’d home.
Here shall the ARTS in glorious splendour shine!
And AGRICULTURE give her stores divine!
COMMERCE refin’d, dispense us more than gold,
And this new world, teach WISDOM to the old—
RELIGION here shall fix her blest abode,
Array’d in mildness, like its parent GOD!
JUSTICE and LAW, shall endless PEACE maintain,
And the “SATURNIAN AGE,” return again.
