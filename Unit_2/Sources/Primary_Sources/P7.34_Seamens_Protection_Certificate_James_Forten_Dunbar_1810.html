Primary Source P7.34
Seamen’s Protection Certificate for James Forten Dunbar
Alexander Tod, Alderman, City of Philadelphia · July 12, 1810
 
TOC FIT Chapter 7 · Essay prompt 7.4 (Necessary War or War of Choice? The War of 1812)
WHY HERE A protection certificate shows how Americans tried to prove citizenship against British impressment, and how the same document recorded a Black sailor’s free status.
Historical Context

A 1796 act of Congress allowed American seamen to obtain certificates of citizenship intended to protect them from impressment by the Royal Navy. Local officials recorded the applicant’s age, height, complexion, and identifying marks, and a witness swore to the place of birth.

This Philadelphia certificate was issued to James Forten Dunbar, described as an eleven-year-old “mulatto,” with the sailmaker James Forten, a prominent free Black Philadelphian, swearing to his birth. For Black sailors, such papers also served as proof of free status. The description of marks and complexion shows how officials classified the people they certified.

Editorial note: Transcription from the U.S. Law and Race Initiative OER (University of Nebraska–Lincoln); italics mark words written by hand on the printed form. The image is the National Archives original (RG 36).
Document Text

Philadelphia City, ss.

On the twelfth day of July one thousand eight hundred and ten

Personally came before Alexander Tod, Esq. one of the Aldermen of the City aforesaid, James Forten Dunbar, mulatto aged 11 years, 4 feet seven inches high, with his shoes, yellow complexion, Black hair, 11 years of age, marked mark of inoculation on his left arm, scar on his left thumb, scar on his right shin by the bite of a dog, Born free, being legally sworn, says, that according to the best of his knowledge, he was born in Philadelphia City.

At the same time appeared James Forten being also sworn, says, that he has known and been well acquainted with said James Forten Dunbar for some years, and that according to the best of his knowledge and belief, he the said James Forten Dunbar was born at the place aforesaid. Witness my Hand and Seal, the day and year first written.

James Forten Dunbar
his x mark

James Forten

Sworn before Alexr Tod
Alderman
