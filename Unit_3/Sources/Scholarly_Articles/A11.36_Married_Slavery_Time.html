Selection Overview

Parry opens with Mary Ashton Rice, a Boston schoolteacher on a Virginia plantation in 1839, who watched the broomstick wedding of Pompey and Susan led by Uncle Aaron, an elderly enslaved man known for healing and conjure. Rice dismissed it as futile, but Uncle Aaron insisted the broomstick was as binding as a preacher, and Parry traces the ritual's origins across the Atlantic world.
