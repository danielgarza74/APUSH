Selection Overview

Quist examines the subscription list of the Signal of Liberty, Michigan's Liberty party newspaper, to identify antislavery's rural rank and file. Most subscribers, he finds, were cash-crop farmers who were appalled by slavery yet optimistic about the growing market economy. He reviews earlier scholars who disagreed over whether abolitionists came from prosperous or stagnant farming communities.
