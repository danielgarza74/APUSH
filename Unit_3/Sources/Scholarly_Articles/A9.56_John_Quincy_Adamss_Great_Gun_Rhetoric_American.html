Selection Overview

Weeks examines John Quincy Adams's 1818 letter to George Erving defending Jackson's Seminole War campaign in Florida, long praised as a great state paper. He argues that its self-righteous tone quieted critics at home and abroad and helped produce the Transcontinental Treaty, but also voiced myths of American empire, set within a policy of removing Native Americans from the Southeast.
