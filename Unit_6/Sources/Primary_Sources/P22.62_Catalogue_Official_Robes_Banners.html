Primary Source P22.62
Catalogue of Official Robes and Banners
Knights of the Ku Klux Klan · 1925
 
TOC FIT The Second Klan
WHY HERE The Klan’s own mail-order catalogue of regalia shows the order’s ranks, its national supply business, and what each robe and banner cost.
TREATMENT Public-domain catalogue: one page image and selected text
Historical Context

The Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, Incorporated, published this illustrated catalogue in Atlanta in 1925, the year the second Klan was near its peak. Its opening note explains that the catalogue was prepared because of “the large number of uniforms and other supplies” being made at the order’s own plant, and that one copy went to each Exalted Cyclops, the head of a local Klan, to use when ordering regalia. Each page pairs a color illustration of a robe or banner with a short description of its materials and its price. The copy reproduced here belongs to the George Washington Flowers Memorial Collection at Duke University Library and was digitized by the Internet Archive in 2011.

The second Klan was a white supremacist, nativist, anti-Catholic, and antisemitic organization, and it was also a business. Its national office in Atlanta controlled the sale of official robes, and regalia sales, along with initiation fees, brought money to the national leadership. The titles in the catalogue trace the order’s chain of command, from the ordinary Klansman to the Kleagle (recruiter), the Exalted Cyclops, and the Grand Dragon who headed a state “Realm.” Notice the price range: a plain cotton Klansman’s robe cost $5.00, while satin robes for state and national officers cost $24.00 to $40.00. Notice too that the sample banner names “Red Bank Klan No. 10,” Realm of New Jersey, a sign that the order had spread well beyond the South.

Editorial note. The catalogue is reproduced as historical evidence of how the Klan organized and supplied itself. One page is shown as an image; the opening note and the descriptions on the other pages are transcribed from the page images. Dot leaders before prices are omitted. Banners Nos. 2–4, which repeat the wording and $40.00 price of Banner No. 1 (Nos. 3 and 4 measure 32x52 inches), are omitted, as are a photograph captioned “Robe and Printing Plants, (Buckhead) Atlanta, Georgia” and the blank pages; the robe pages follow in the catalogue’s order. The catalogue has no page numbers.
Primary Source Image and Text
“Klansman” robe, from Catalogue of Official Robes and Banners, 1925. Knights of the Ku Klux Klan. Duke University Libraries, via Internet Archive.

Due to the large number of uniforms and other supplies being manufactured at your plant, your Imperial officers feel that the Klansmen of the nation should be better posted as to these articles, hence this catalogue was prepared.

We have endeavored to give full description of all robes, etc., showing materials used and prices.

Each Exalted Cyclops will be furnished with one copy of catalogue, which should be carefully guarded and kept so it can be referred to at all times in ordering regalia, etc. These books are expensive, therefore each Klan must carefully preserve their copies as our supply is limited.

Your Imperial officers also wish to call to your attention, the fact that we have in Atlanta, a new and modern printing plant, owned and operated by our organization; so equipped as to enable us to do printing and binding for local Klans at a worth-while saving to them. We will welcome inquiries and gladly quote prices on any printing local Klans might need.
