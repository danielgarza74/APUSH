Knights of the Ku Klux Klan · May 9, 1923
 
TOC FIT The Second Klan; Reactionary Politics and Immigration Restriction
WHY HERE Short news items from the Klan’s national weekly show how the order reported its growth, its finances and building plans, and its campaigns on schools, charities, and employers to its own members in 1923.
TREATMENT Public-domain text, selected articles
Historical Context

The Imperial Night-Hawk was the official weekly of the Knights of the Ku Klux Klan, published from the order’s national headquarters, which it called the “Imperial Palace,” in Atlanta. It began in the spring of 1923; this is its sixth issue, dated May 9, 1923. Written for Klan members, its short items report on local Klans and on the state organizations the order called “Realms,” announce the doings of national officers, and tell members what to watch for in their own towns. In November 1922 Hiram W. Evans, a Dallas dentist, had replaced the founder, William Joseph Simmons, as Imperial Wizard; Simmons kept the honorary title of Emperor. The scan shows a color cover of a robed rider carrying a fiery cross.

The second Klan was a white supremacist, nativist, anti-Catholic, and antisemitic organization, and in 1923 it was growing fastest outside the South, in states such as Indiana and Oregon as well as Texas. The items here present its aims as civic duty: supporting a bill to bar noncitizens from teaching school, urging members to make sure Protestant charities got their share of community funds, protesting textbooks said to favor the Catholic church, and celebrating Oregon’s 1922 election, in which Klan-backed candidates won office. Notice also the story about Alfred C. Fuller of the Fuller Brush Company: the paper presents a business owner’s retraction, after members learned of his threat to fire Klansmen, as proof of the members’ power.

Editorial note. This issue is reproduced as historical evidence of how the Klan spoke to its own members; its claims about membership, motives, and events are the Klan’s, not established fact. Selected articles are given under their own headlines, and omitted items are marked. The omissions include a lurid crime story used to promote a Protestant girls’ home, an anti-Catholic anecdote, and the issue’s remaining pages. The text was transcribed from the page images with the help of machine OCR; letters lost in the shadow of the binding were restored from the images. The brackets in Fuller’s letter appear as printed. “Lauguages” is the paper’s own misprint.

Fuller Who Fought Klan Now Retracts

Alfred C. Fuller, president of the Fuller Brush Company, has had a change in heart.

A few weeks ago he was actively attacking the Knights of the Ku Klux Klan and giving out newspaper interviews to the effect that any Klansman in the employ of his company would be discharged.

Now Mr. Fuller has backed down. Since Klansmen of the nation were informed of his attitude through the columns of the Imperial Night-Hawk, for some reason or the other Fuller has changed his mind.

To the head of the Klan in the State of Connecticut, G. H. Abercrombie, secretary of the Fuller Brush Company, has written saying that Mr. Fuller wished a retraction published and says:

“Mr. Fuller is writing you direct on the matter and has drafted a letter, which I believe adequately states our true present and future attitude in this regard.

“I trust you will see your way clear to have Mr. Fuller’s letter given such publicity as will go a long way toward relieving the feeling that exists in many quarters over this situation.”

“March 21, 1923. To the King Kleagle of Connecticut and Rhode Island.

“Dear Sir: [Certain statements concerning [the Ku] Klux Klan have been attributed to me, and certain ones I have made personally. Regardless of this, however, I wish to state that my present attitude and future policy in this regard is absolutely a neutral one. I wish here also to make the definite statement that any member of our organization belonging to the Klan will not be discharged. This is and will be our attitude concerning such a condition, regardless of previous statements. The Fuller Brush Company has never concerned itself as to whether its employees belonged to any particular sect, creed, religion, fraternity or political party. Such has been its policy in the past and such will be its policy in the future. Furthermore, the Company believes in the absolute independence of thought on religious, fraternal and political matters on the part of the people connected with it. Work and loyalty to our organization are our chief interests—not the nature of one’s beliefs.

“Yours very sincerely,

“(Signed) THE FULLER BRUSH CO.

“By Alfred C. Fuller,

“President.”

So, you see, Mr. Fuller did not mean all that he said.

Would Bar Aliens As School Teachers

The legislature of the State of Minnesota has up for consideration a bill which might well be introduced in every legislature in the United States in the cause of better Americanism.

The bill would prohibit any person not a citizen of the United States from teaching in any elementary school in Minnesota.

Children in the elementary schools should be safeguarded against un-American or foreign interests. It is at this period of life that the child should be taught patriotism and love of country. Such ideas cannot be properly inculcated in formative minds by individuals who do not render whole-souled devotion to flag and country themselves and whose hearts are not thoroughly in accord with American ideals and principles.

Should Scrutinize Charity Allotments

Many drives for the purpose of raising funds for organized charities are being conducted in cities in all parts of the United States.

It would be well for Klansmen to carefully check the disposition of such funds and be sure that Protestant institutions receive their proper proportion of the money donated for charitable work.

The Tri-State American, a newspaper of Memphis, Tenn., recently analyzed the Charity Community Fund of that city and found that while Catholic, Jewish and Negro institutions were allotted $121,000, the Protestant institutions, including both the Young Men’s Christian Association and Young Women’s Christian Association, were to receive only $56,000. One Protestant institution, The American Christian Home, received no allotment at all. The Convent of the Good Shepherd and St. Peters Orphanage, Catholic institutions, were to receive between them $76,898—$20,000 more than the Protestant total.

Klans in Western States Are Growing

The Knights of the Ku Klux Klan is at present experiencing an unparalleled growth in the northwestern states and membership will still further increase during the coming summer, according to Fred L. Gifford of Portland, Oregon, Grand Dragon for that Realm. Mr. Gifford was a visitor at the Imperial Palace May 1 on official business.

“The Klan in the Western States has a great mission of Americanism to perform,” Mr. Gifford says. “The rapid growth of the Japanese population and the great influx of foreign laborers, mostly Greeks, is threatening our American institutions, and Klans in Washington, Oregon and Idaho are actively at work to combat these foreign and un-American influences.”

Klans in the state of Oregon are rested up after their recent sweeping victory at the polls in the cause of good government, at which time they put patriotic Americans into public office. During the coming summer there will be a great increase in Klan membership in these States. Thousands of Americans are anxious to enroll themselves in this great patriotic order and the very best of the Western citizenship will be accepted.

In Portland, Oregon, Luther Powell Klan Number 1, is making plans for the erection of a magnificent new Klavern which they will themselves own. Other Klans in the western states are also preparing to construct their own buildings.

Texas Klan Outlines An Educational Program

Constructive and educational programs for the benefit of the membership have caused a wonderful attendance record to be established by Wichita Falls Klan No. 195, Realm of Texas.

This Klan has purchased an ideal building site for the sum of $14,000 and is going to at once erect thereon a $35,000 Klavern paid for and owned by Klansmen.

Wichita Falls Klan each week has some special educational subject presented for discussion. Some of the subjects which will be discussed are printed here as a suggestion for other Klans:

“The Constitution of the United States”; “The Magna Carta”; “Immigration”; “The Public Schools”; “State and Church”; “The History of the Knights of the Ku Klux Klan”; “The State and the School”; “The Monroe Doctrine”; “The Revolutionary War,”; “Abraham Lincoln”; “George Washington”; “American Statesmen”; “The Catholic Church”; “Our Congress.”

These lectures are generally delivered by local Klansmen specially assigned to the subject. They are followed by a general discussion.

Klan Officials of Indiana in Meeting

On Sunday, April 29, a most remarkable meeting of Klansmen was held in the city of Indianapolis, Ind. It was composed entirely of officials from several hundred Indiana Klans, and two thousand men were present. It was the largest meeting of state officials in the history of the Knights of the Ku Klux Klan.

Imperial Wizard Dr. H. W. Evans addressed the gathering, congratulating the State of Indiana upon the marvellous progress which it has recently made in Klankraft. Plans were formulated by the enthusiastic Klan leaders for a still greater increase in the numerical strength of the Indiana Klans during the summer months.

Indiana is now running close to Texas as being the strongest Klan state in the union.

There are over 1,200 daily, weekly and monthly publications in foreign lauguages at present being printed and circulated in the United States. This alien press is a deterrent to American citizenship.

Klansmen Should Scan Text Books

Klansmen, guard well your free public schools and let no un-American influences creep in.

Pay particular attention to the contents of text books adopted for the use of your children by either state, county or city commissions or boards of education, lest sectarianism be skillfully introduced.

The Klans of the Realm of Indiana are now protesting the adoption of several text books which they find to be full of Roman Catholic theories and the Board of Education of that state have temporarily held up the book contracts.

Two history books in particular were found to be “chuck” full of doctrine and propaganda favorable to the Roman Catholic church. One of the books carried as its preface an endorsement by a priest.

Take a look at the youngsters’ school books when they bring them home. Maybe you will find the hierarchy at work.
