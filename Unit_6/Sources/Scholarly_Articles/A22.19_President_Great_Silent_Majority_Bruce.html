EXCERPT Original journal pages 593-594
JSTOR https://www.jstor.org/stable/1559844
Selection Overview

Buckley argues that adman Bruce Barton crafted Coolidge's public image, applying consumer-advertising methods to politics. Barton sold an image of authenticity to Americans unsettled by modern change, signaling a shift in American political culture.
