Irvine H., Jr. Anderson
 
ARTICLE The 1941 De Facto Embargo on Oil to Japan: A Bureaucratic Reflex

EXCERPT Original journal pages 201-202
JSTOR https://www.jstor.org/stable/3638003
Selection Overview

Anderson argues that Roosevelt did not intend to cut off Japan's oil when he froze funds in July 1941 and wanted shipments to continue; a biased bureaucracy produced an unplanned de facto embargo later ratified, which pushed Japan toward seizing the Indies.
