Keynes and the German Inflation
Niall Ferguson
 
ARTICLE Keynes and the German Inflation
JOURNAL The English Historical Review 110, no. 436 (1995)
EXCERPT Original journal pages 368-369
JSTOR https://www.jstor.org/stable/576013
Selection Overview

Ferguson argues that Keynes advised Hamburg businessmen and German governments more closely than assumed, and that his advice was bad: although the reparations were excessive, he miscalculated their consequences and encouraged policies that fed hyperinflation.
