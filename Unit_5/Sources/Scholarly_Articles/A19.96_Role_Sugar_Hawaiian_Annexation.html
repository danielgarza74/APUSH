Selection Overview

Russ revisits the long dispute over whether sugar interests caused the January 17, 1893, Hawaiian revolution. Some planters wanted annexation to gain a bounty, he notes, while many opposed it for fear of losing their labor supply. Though he accepts parts of Pratt's account, he argues Pratt missed the links between sugar, immigration, and racial friction involving Chinese and Japanese residents.
