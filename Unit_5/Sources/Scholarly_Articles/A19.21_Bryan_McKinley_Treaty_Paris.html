To Bryan, expansion per se was antagonistic to the ideals America had cherished since the days of the Declaration of Independence. Expansio implied that the United States would become involved in the quarrels of Europe and Asia, thereby scrapping the Monroe Doctrine, and in wars for the subjugation of alien races. Most important, colonialism meant the abandonment of the vital principle of government only b the consent of the governed.
