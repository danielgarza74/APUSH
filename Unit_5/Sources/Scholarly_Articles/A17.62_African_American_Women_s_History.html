Selection Overview

Higginbotham argues that feminist theory has said little about race, even though questions of difference and power long stood at the core of Black scholarship. She calls on historians of African-American women to treat race as a metalanguage that shapes how gender, class, and sexuality are constructed and represented, and as a contested tool of both oppression and liberation.
