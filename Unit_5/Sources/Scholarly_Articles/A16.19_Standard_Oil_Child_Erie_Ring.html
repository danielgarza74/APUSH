NOTES AND DOCUMENTS THE STANDARD OIL, CHILD OF THE ERIE RING, 1868-1872. SIX CONTRACTS AND A LETTER By CHESTER MCARTHTJR DESTLER The seven manuscripts reproduced below are related to an important but neglected chapter of American industrial history. They were found scattered among the legal papers of Roger Sherman of Titusville, Pennsylvania, a leader of the bar in the Oil Region before the turn of the century. As attorney at various times for the Allegheny Transportation Company, the indepen- dent producers and refiners, and the Standard Oil, he gathered what is perhaps the most important single collection of mate- rials bearing upon the history of the oil industry between 1868 and his death in 1897. Thanks to his daughter, Mrs. Thomas W. Phillips, Jr., and now to her husband after her death, these pa- pers have been preserved. It is to the great courtesy of Thomas W. Phillips, Jr., that I am indebted for permission to publish the six contracts and the accompanying letter that appear below. When discovered these manuscripts were carefully tested in order to make certain of their authenticity. Comparison of the handwriting in which the several contracts were drawn revealed that the one dated June 4, 1868, between the Allegheny Trans- portation Company and W. C. Andrews, 0. H. Payne, and H. M. Flagler of Cleveland, Ohio, was drafted unquestionably by Roger Sherman. Sealed by the Secretary of the Allegheny Com- pany, witnessed, executed as a.ttested by the United States In- ternal Revenue Certificate "5" attached to the pa.per, the au- thenticity of this document is unquestionable. The signature of Henry Harley, President of the Allegheny, which is appended to this contract, is identical with the same signature on the other five contracts.
