Selection Overview

Smith examines how McKinley explained his decision to acquire the Philippines, beginning with an 1898 interview recorded by Chandler Anderson that he calls McKinley's earliest detailed statement on the subject. He also weighs James Rusling's widely cited 1899 account of McKinley praying for guidance, urging caution about its reliability and noting similar recollections by other visitors such as Schurman.
