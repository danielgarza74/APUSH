Selection Overview

Osborne notes that most historians credit the Spanish-American War with Hawaii's annexation, treating trade with the Orient and racial strife with Japan as background. He contends instead that expanding American trade, especially with the Far East, was the primary reason the islands were annexed, and faults LaFeber and McCormick for asserting rather than demonstrating that commercial case.
