Selection Overview

Robinson describes Virginia's Indian confederacies and argues that the tributary system, grounded in treaties such as that of 1677, brought mutual benefit, with protection, land and educational privileges for tributary tribes. He holds that it modifies, if it does not refute completely, the generalization that for the English the only good Indian was a dead Indian.
