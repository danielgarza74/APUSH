EXCERPT Original journal pages 79-80
JSTOR https://www.jstor.org/stable/365221
Selection Overview

Ranlet reexamines contemporary accounts of King Philip's War, criticizes both nineteenth-century racist writing and New Left sympathy for the Indians, and concludes that the timing of the 1675 war was probably not Philip's choice and that Philip appears to have been a cautious leader of his people.
