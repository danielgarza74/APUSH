New Haven and the Stamp Act Crisis of 1765–66
David Alan Richards

JOURNAL The Yale University Library Gazette 46, no. 2 (1971)
EXCERPT Original journal pages 67-68
JSTOR https://www.jstor.org/stable/40858320
Selection Overview

Richards studies how New Haven, Connecticut, took part in the Stamp Act controversy of 1765-66, including the role of Benjamin Mecom's Connecticut Gazette and local conservative and “New Light” factions tied to Yale. He ends with a perceptive contemporary's warning that the Stamp Act had laid the foundations of an independent state.
