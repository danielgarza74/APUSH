Selection Overview

Greene and Jellison argue that historians, concentrating on taxation and the mercantile system, have neglected the Currency Act of 1764, which banned new legal tender paper money in the colonies south of New England. They find that it did not break sharply with earlier imperial policy and that in many colonies it was an underlying source of discontent during the last decade of the colonial period.
