Robert E. Shalhope
 
ARTICLE Toward a Republican Synthesis: The Emergence of an Understanding of Republicanism in American Historiography
JOURNAL The William and Mary Quarterly 29, no. 1 (1972)
EXCERPT Original journal pages 49-50
JSTOR https://www.jstor.org/stable/1921327
Selection Overview

Shalhope argues that the formula "Locke et praeterea nihil" obscured the vital shaping role of republicanism in early American thought, and that only within the last decade have historians clearly discerned its distinctive qualities in the period 1760 to 1789. He traces the evolution of these ideas to sketch a tentative "republican synthesis." (historiography)
