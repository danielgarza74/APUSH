Selection Overview

Berson takes up the suggestion that the Stono rebels of 1739 had just heard of rising hostilities between Britain and Spain and sought to escape to Spanish Saint Augustine. He presents evidence of when and how that news reached Charles Town and concludes it is likely the rebels heard it shortly before the revolt began.
