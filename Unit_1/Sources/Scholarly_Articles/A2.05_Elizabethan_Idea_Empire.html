Selection Overview

Armitage argues that the English idea of empire in Elizabeth’s reign was derivative, belated and incoherent, drawn from classical rather than indigenous sources and arising more than a century after the Scots had elaborated their own. He adds that its backwardness and isolation mirrored England’s failure to found permanent Atlantic settlements.
