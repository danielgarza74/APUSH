Selection Overview

Wright surveys why England was so late to colonize North America, noting that Spain had built a vast empire nearly a century before England gained a foothold. He concludes that fear of offending Spain and the lack of investment capital held back colonial undertakings until the defeat of the Armada and the growth of London merchants' capital moved the initiative from sea captains and courtiers to stock companies.
